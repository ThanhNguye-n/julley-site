#Install

  1. Get Julley from the download page and open the file.
  2. Drag Julley into Applications, then open it from there.
  3. Julley lives in the menu bar (the coral loop), not in the Dock. A short setup walks you through permissions and sign-in.

Julley needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

#The two permissions

macOS asks you to switch these on yourself, in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Julley’s setup window opens the right page for you.

Accessibility
Lets Julley read the names and positions of the buttons and menus in the app you’re using. Julley only reads them. It never clicks, types or scrolls.
Screen Recording
Lets Julley look at the front window when you ask, for apps whose buttons it can’t read. Nothing is recorded in the background. macOS applies this switch after Julley restarts, so the setup window offers Restart Julley once you’ve been to Settings.
Microphone and Speech Recognition (optional)
Only if you want to ask out loud.

#Asking

Press ⌥ Space in any app, type your question and press Return. To talk instead, turn on Ask by voice in Settings → General, then hold ⌥ Space, speak and let go. You can pick another shortcut in Settings → General.

AskJulley
“Where is dark mode?”Circles it, with an arrow and a short label. If it’s in a menu, it guides you through the menu.
“How do I share my screen?”Numbered steps, one at a time. It waits for your click before showing the next.
“What matters in this email?”Highlights the important lines and sums it up in one sentence.
“Translate the buttons into Vietnamese”Puts a translation under each label.
Select some text, then “what does this mean?”Answers about the selected text, without a picture.
“Shortcuts?” or “what does ⌘K do?”Every keyboard shortcut in the app’s menus, on one sheet you can filter. Instant, and it doesn’t use up an answer.

Right after an answer, press the shortcut again to ask a follow-up about the same thing. Press ⌫ in the empty field, or ✕, to start fresh. ↑ brings back your last question.

Name the thing you want: “where do I turn on captions?” works better than “help”.

#What you’ll see

  • A circle and an arrow point at the thing, with a label. Click it and the marks turn green and fade. Press Esc, click somewhere else or scroll to clear them.
  • Menus. Julley doesn’t open menus for you. It points at the menu to open, then the item to hover, then the entry, and shows its keyboard shortcut for next time.
  • Steps come one at a time, numbered. Julley waits for your click before it shows the next one (up to 12).
  • “Probably here — checking…” In apps Julley can’t read, a grey first guess appears while it looks at the picture. The real answer replaces it.
  • Share. ⌥ ⌘ S while an answer is on screen copies a picture of the window with Julley’s marks, and saves it to Pictures › Julley.

#Privacy in practice

  • Julley looks only when you ask. It sends your question, the names of the buttons and labels in the front window and, only when needed, a picture of that window.
  • Activity (menu bar → Activity) shows exactly what was sent for each of your last 50 questions. Turn it off or clear it in Settings → Privacy.
  • Quick look asks a small decision model first, so simple “where is…?” questions are answered in about half a second. Turn it off in Settings → Advanced.
  • The details, including who processes what, are in the privacy policy.

#Plans and your account

  • Free: 20 answers a day, reset at midnight UTC. Instant answers, when your words match a button exactly, don’t count.
  • Pro: $6 a month or $48 a year, up to 2,000 answers a month. A step-by-step guide counts once. See pricing.
  • Upgrade, change plan, update your card or cancel from your account page (Manage billing).
  • Delete your account in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account…) or on the account page. If you’re on Pro, the subscription is cancelled first.

#Fixing the usual hiccups

“Julley can’t see your screen yet”, although the switches are on
Restart Julley. Still stuck? In System Settings → Privacy & Security, select Julley under Accessibility and under Screen Recording, press − to remove it, then + to add it back, and restart Julley. macOS sometimes treats an updated app as a new one.
The shortcut does nothing
Another app may use the same keys; input-source switchers and launchers often take ⌃ Space or ⌘ Space. Pick another shortcut in Settings → General.
Julley points at the wrong thing
Ask again with the words you see on screen, or say where it is (“in the toolbar”, “in the File menu”). Answers come from AI models and can be wrong. Julley only points; the click is always yours. To tell us, open Activity and use Report a problem: it drafts an email you can read before sending.
The sign-in link opens the browser, not Julley
That’s expected with some mail apps: the page has an Open Julley button. Links work once and expire after 15 minutes, so ask for a new one if needed.
“That’s today’s 20 free answers”
Free answers come back at midnight UTC. Pro gives you up to 2,000 a month.

Still stuck? Email hello@askjulley.com. A screenshot helps.

#Uninstall

  1. If you want your account gone too, delete it first: Settings → Account → Delete account…, or on the account page any time.
  2. Quit Julley (menu bar → the power button) and drag it from Applications to the Trash.
  3. To remove what it kept on this Mac: the folders ~/Library/Application Support/Julley (Activity) and ~/Pictures/Julley (pictures you shared), and Julley’s entries under Accessibility and Screen Recording in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

#Keyboard shortcuts

⌥ SpaceAsk: tap to type, hold to talk
ReturnSend the question
↑Your last question
⌫ in the empty fieldDrop the follow-up and ask something new
EscClose the question, or clear Julley’s marks
⌥ ⌘ SShare the answer on screen
⌘ 1 – ⌘ 4Settings tabs: General, Account, Privacy, Advanced

The app you’re in

Ask “shortcuts?”, or open the Julley menu and click Shortcuts in …, to see every shortcut in that app’s menus. Type to filter by name (“tab”) or by keys (“⌘T”, “cmd shift n”). ↑ ↓ move, Return or a click shows where the entry is in the menu, Esc closes. Julley reads the menus on your Mac and never presses the keys for you.

Julley only looks. It never clicks, types or takes over your Mac.
