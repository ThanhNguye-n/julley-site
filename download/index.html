Download for macOS

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Releases are published on GitHub.

Up and running in a minute

  1. Download and open it

    Open the file you just downloaded and drag Julley into your Applications folder.

  2. Start Julley

    It lives in your menu bar — there’s no Dock window. A short setup walks you through the rest.

  3. Grant two permissions and sign in

    Julley shows live status as you switch each one on, then emails you a sign-in link. No password.

  4. Try it

    Press ⌥Space and ask “Where is the Settings button?” on the practice window.

Two permissions — both for looking

macOS asks you to approve each one. Julley never asks for anything it would need to click or type for you.

Accessibility Required

Why: so Julley can read the names and positions of buttons, menus and text in the window you ask about — that’s how it finds the right spot fast. It also notices when you press Esc, click or scroll, so it can clear its marks and look again after you act.

What Julley doesn’t do with it: press, type or change anything for you.

System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility

Screen Recording Required

Why: so Julley can take one picture of the window you asked about — only when reading it isn’t enough, for example in apps that draw their own screens.

What it doesn’t do: record video or watch your screen in the background. A picture is taken only when you ask a question, and Julley doesn’t keep it. Without this permission, Julley is limited to instant matches on your Mac.

System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording (called “Screen & System Audio Recording” on newer macOS)

Want to ask out loud? macOS will also ask for microphone access the first time you hold ⌥ Space to talk. That one is optional. You can switch any permission off later in System Settings.

What you’ll need

  • A Mac running macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later
  • An internet connection — Julley’s answers come from the cloud
  • An email address to sign in with (no password)
