Pricing questions

What counts toward the free limit?

Only questions that need a picture of your screen or a web search. Questions Julley answers from the text on screen — skim, translate, “what does this mean?”, where is…, step-by-step guides — are unlimited. A question counts once, however many steps or retries it takes.

What happens when I use my 10 picture questions?

Julley answers from the text on screen when it can, and tells you when a question really needs a picture. The count resets at 00:00 UTC. Or go Pro for unlimited.

What does “fair use” mean?

Pro covers up to 2,000 picture and search questions a month — about 65 a day. Text questions are unlimited on every plan; a very high daily cap only stops automated abuse.

Who handles payment?

Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. Checkout opens in your browser, and Lemon Squeezy takes care of VAT and sales tax. Julley never sees your card details.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Open Manage billing on your account page and cancel. Pro stays active until the end of the period you’ve paid for, then you move back to Free.

Is there a team plan?

Not yet. If you’d like Julley for a group, get in touch and tell us what you need.

Julley only looks. It never clicks, types or takes over your Mac.
