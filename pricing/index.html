Pricing questions

What happens when I use my 20 free answers?

Julley tells you you’ve reached today’s limit. Instant answers still work, and the count resets at 00:00 UTC. Or go Pro for more.

What does “fair use” mean on Pro?

Pro covers up to 2,000 answers a month — about 65 a day, resetting each month. That’s more than almost anyone needs, so in practice it’s unlimited.

What’s an “answer” again?

One question that needed the AI. When your words exactly match a button or menu on screen, Julley points instantly on your Mac and that doesn’t count.

Who handles payment?

Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. Checkout opens in your browser, and Lemon Squeezy takes care of VAT and sales tax. Julley never sees your card details.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Open Manage billing on your account page and cancel. Pro stays active until the end of the period you’ve paid for, then you move back to Free.

Is there a team plan?

Not yet. If you’d like Julley for a group, get in touch and tell us what you need.

Julley only looks. It never clicks, types or takes over your Mac.
