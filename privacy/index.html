Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

The short version. Julley looks at your screen only when you ask it a question. It never clicks, types or takes over your Mac. It doesn’t keep your screenshots. To answer, Julley’s server passes your question and what Julley saw to an AI model provider, and sends the answer back.

Who we are

Julley is a Mac app and this website, made by a small independent team. You can reach us at hello@askjulley.com.

What Julley reads, and when

  • Only when you ask. Julley does nothing with your screen until you press the shortcut and ask a question. There’s no background watching and no continuous recording.
  • The window’s labels first. Julley reads the names, roles and positions of buttons, menus and text that the app in front exposes to macOS (the accessibility tree).
  • A picture only if needed. If the labels aren’t enough, Julley takes one screenshot of the part of the screen you’re working in, usually the active window, scaled down. Julley’s own overlay and prompt are left out of it.
  • Chat questions read a little further back. When you ask about a group chat (“what did I miss?”, “did anyone ask me?”), Julley also reads the older messages just above what’s on screen, up to about 8,000 characters, so it can catch you up. They are sent with that question only.
  • Esc, clicks and scrolls. Julley notices when you press Esc, click or scroll, only so it can clear its marks and look again after you act. It doesn’t record or store what you type.
  • It never acts. Julley doesn’t click, type, scroll, open apps or change settings on your Mac. When it needs more, it asks you to do it.

  • Voice. If you ask out loud, your Mac’s speech recognition turns it into text — on your Mac when your Mac supports that, otherwise through Apple’s speech service. Julley’s server receives the text, not an audio recording.
  • Instant answers. When your words exactly match a label on screen, Julley points straight away on your Mac and sends nothing.
  • Payments. Card details go to Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider and merchant of record. We never see them. We receive your plan status and renewal date.

Who processes it

  • Julley’s server runs on Cloudflare. It checks your sign-in, counts your usage and forwards each question to the model provider. It doesn’t save the content of your questions, the labels or the screenshots.
  • AI model providers receive each question to produce the answer. Requests go through OpenRouter to the models Julley uses (today, models from Anthropic and OpenAI, and TypeSafe’s Jev for Quick look, which receives your question and the names of the controls; in apps Julley can’t read, it also receives the window picture sent for that question, to draw a first guess while the main model answers. You can turn Quick look off in Settings → Advanced). They process the content under their own terms and privacy policies. Julley’s server doesn’t add your email address or account details to the request.
  • Email provider delivers your sign-in links.
  • Lemon Squeezy handles checkout, taxes and the billing portal.

We don’t sell your data and we don’t show ads. We don’t use your screenshots or questions to build profiles or train our own models.

Your choices

  • See what was sent. Open Activity in the menu bar to see the exact text and screenshot behind each recent answer.
  • Clear it. Clear Activity at any time.
  • Turn off a permission. Switch off Accessibility or Screen Recording in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Julley then does less, or nothing.
  • Delete your account. In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account, or use your account page. We delete your account, sign-in sessions, usage records and subscription records right away. If you’re on Pro, the subscription is cancelled first so you’re not charged again. Lemon Squeezy, which handles payments, keeps its own invoices as tax law requires. You can also email hello@askjulley.com from your sign-in address.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to some processing. Write to us and we’ll help.

Cookies and this website

This website sets no cookies and has no ad or analytics trackers. When you sign in on the account page, it keeps a session token in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in; “Sign out” removes it. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party. The account page talks to Julley’s server to sign you in and show your plan and usage.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Sign-in links work once and expire after 15 minutes, and we store only hashes of sign-in and session tokens. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold small on purpose.

Children

Julley isn’t aimed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change how Julley handles data in a way that matters, we’ll update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app for anything significant.
